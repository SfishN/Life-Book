Status: Planned; no external sessions completed

## Research Questions

1. Can participants understand the room and furniture mappings without coaching?
2. Can they create a quest, skill, task, annotation, and reflection?
3. Do carried tasks feel supportive rather than punitive?
4. Do participants understand what the Mirror can and cannot decide/save?
5. Does keeping the room visible behind panels add meaning or visual distraction?

## Participants

- Target: 5 participants who already use some form of planning, journaling, or self-reflection.
- Include a range of game familiarity and keyboard confidence.
- Do not recruit vulnerable users for mental-health claims; this prototype is not care.

## Session Tasks

1. Enter the room and explain what you think it is.
2. Find where you would create an important life direction.
3. Create a related skill and a small task for today.
4. Use Calendar to annotate the day and edit the task.
5. Find the carried task and describe how the wording feels.
6. Complete a task and record why it mattered.
7. Speak with the Mirror and explain what you think will be saved.
8. Find the Life Novel and describe its purpose.

## Measures

- Task success without help
- Time and wrong turns per task
- Furniture-mapping comprehension
- Trust/comfort rating (1-5)
- Perceived pressure rating (1-5; lower is better)
- AI boundary comprehension
- Qualitative quotes, with consent
