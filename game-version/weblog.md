## Description
1/8/2026

The project called life novel is designed as a self-management tool under the RPG game style and with the AI accompany. Aiming to let users witness their progress of life directly. The introduction to AI is intended to judge a skill, make a plan/goal, release users' stress. The function desgined in the project includes to-do list, sucess diary, main quest, skill tree, calendar, character info, ai accompany and life novel. According to users' daily record, each week will give a monthly novel, so peace life makes stories.

*Life Book is an AI-powered personal growth app that transforms the real-life journey of self-improvement into an RPG adventure. Users no longer simply manage to-do lists; instead, they take on the role of the protagonist in their own lives, setting long-term goals, developing skill trees, advancing through main quests, collecting achievements, and continuously growing through real-world actions.*

*The app features an AI guide, similar to the beginner NPC that accompanies players at the start of an RPG. It doesn’t make decisions for users, but rather helps them break down goals, assess their progress, provide feedback, and record important moments worth remembering.
Each day, users are free to choose between “To-Do List” or “Success Journal” to document their day. The AI uses this content to update skill trees and growth progress, while identifying key milestones the user has achieved.*

*Every month, the AI automatically generates a personalized “Book of Life” for each user, weaving the real events of the month into an RPG-style narrative that chronicles growth, challenges, significant people, achievements, and life-changing moments. The problem *Life Book* aims to solve isn’t “how to improve efficiency,” but how to help people see their own growth. It hopes to give everyone the experience of being “the protagonist of their own life”—where every effort and every breakthrough in real life ultimately becomes their own story.*
