## Definition of Ready

- User problem and desired outcome are clear.
- Dependencies and privacy implications are known.
- Acceptance criteria are testable.
- Discovery evidence exists for high-cost or high-risk work.

## Definition of Done

- Acceptance criteria pass.
- Tests and relevant documents are updated.
- Privacy/AI changes have been reviewed.
- User-facing behavior is verified on desktop and mobile.
- A push occurs only when explicitly requested by the product owner.
